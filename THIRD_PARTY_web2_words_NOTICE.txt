app/src/main/assets/words.txt is the web2 word list (Webster's Second New International Dictionary, 1934), as
published in FreeBSD's source tree at share/dict/web2 (https://github.com/freebsd/freebsd-src), lowercased,
deduplicated and sorted. FreeBSD's README for the list says: "The 1934 copyright has lapsed, according to the
supplier." TellWell uses it only to tell English words from names, so that a name is never put in place of a real
word.
