TellWell Multilingual's standard Hindi cleanup model is a modified version of Gemma 3 1B (instruction-tuned).

Gemma is provided under and subject to the Gemma Terms of Use found at ai.google.dev/gemma/terms

Modifications: the vocabulary was trimmed to the tokens Hindi and English dictation use, the model was fully
fine-tuned for Hindi dictation cleanup, and the weights were quantized to 8 bits (Q8_0, GGUF).
